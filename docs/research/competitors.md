## 2. What their users complain about

1. **Per-tracked-user pricing (Canny).** Canny itself: "the number of people leaving you feedback" is the value metric; auto-upgrade in steps of 100 (https://canny.io/blog/new-pricing/, June 17 2025). Capterra reviewers (surfaced via search, Dec 2025 / Jan 2026): objected to paying for free-trial users; "disliked the new pricing structure"; steep Pro-to-Business jump; Pro caps at 10 managers (https://www.capterra.com/p/161103/Canny/reviews/ , not fetchable, 403). Competitor rhetoric: "That's not a feedback tool. That's a growth tax." (UserJot founder, https://dev.to/shayy/canny-changed-their-pricing-again-and-it-got-even-more-expensive-9dh, 2025-05-22; biased). Vendor-reported ladder: ~$661/mo at 1,000 tracked users (Pro monthly).
2. **Per-seat + metered AI (Featurebase, Dec 2025 change).** Seat pricing and $0.49 per AI resolution make the bill "harder to predict than a flat subscription" (summary of G2 reviewers in https://formbricks.com/blog/featurebase-pricing, competitor). Pay for the support suite even if you only want a board. Legacy customers kept old prices (Dec 4 2025 cutoff).
3. **Aggressive plan walls/cheap-tier gaps.** Branding removal as a separate $69/mo (Featurebase), Quackback $59/mo; Frill Startup capped at 50 ideas; Userback Free hides feedback after 7 days; Nolt/Frill/Upvoty offer no free tier.
4. **Outside-the-app experience.** Canny docs: "The widget is an advanced feature. For most cases, we recommend using your full Canny site" (https://developers.canny.io/install/widget/web). Comparison blogs argue portals "route users outside the app or through an embedded iframe" (pickafeature, vendor opinion). Voter sign-in friction: "a board customers have to sign in to use" (UserJot, vendor). Indie suggestion that GitHub Discussions get more engagement because no new account is needed (Peeqback post).
5. **No mobile SDKs** from the big boards; only WishKit/features.vote (Swift) and pick a feature (Flutter) cover native. No RN SDK found anywhere (one claim contradicted itself).
6. **Closing the loop is weak.** "no way to talk to the customer behind the vote" (UserJot, vendor). Canny reviewers: weak progress updates, mis-categorised requests, weak reporting, hard to get data out, setup effort (G2/Capterra snippets).
7. **Weak/ageing OSS.** Astuto archived Feb 2026; Fider basic (no changelog/roadmap/AI).
8. **Setup effort / bundle overhead** for agent-era teams: reviewers say info is hard to get out "especially for AI tools" (G2 snippet), the exact hole MCP fills.

(No first-hand Reddit/HN/X threads were retrievable; do a manual pass.)

## 3. Pricing benchmarks

- **Entry paid price for indie-oriented tools:** $6 (SeggWat), $9 (features.vote), $13 (Sleekplan yearly), $15 (WishKit, Upvoty, FeedBear yearly), $19 (ProductLift, Peeqback, Supahub, Canny Core, FeedBear), $24-29 (LogChimp, Quackback, Fider Pro, UserJot, Featurebase seat, Nolt, Frill $25). Median "serious" indie entry: about $19-29/mo.
- **Real mid-tier:** $49-59 (Frill Business, Upvoty Hyper, UserJot Pro, Canny Pro $79).
- **Yearly discount norm:** "2 months free" (~17%) is the majority pattern (FeedBear, Supahub, Sleekplan, features.vote, featureOS ~17%, UserVoice 17%). Others: 20% (Canny, Featurebase, Feedbucket, LogChimp up to 20%), 10% (Upvoty), WishKit 17% ($12.50 vs $15).
- **Trials:** 10 days (Featurebase, Supahub, Nolt), 14 days (most), 30 days (Sleekplan, UserVoice).
- **Startup discounts are common:** Featurebase 80-86% off for 3 years, featureOS 6 months free, Userback 25%, nonprofits 30-50%.
- **Lifetime/launch tactics:** Sleekplan and Frill both ran AppSumo lifetime deals for the Business plan (both now sold out/unavailable; https://appsumo.com/products/sleekplan/ , https://appsumo.com/products/frill/). Both vendors survived and moved to subscriptions (Frill still sells; Sleekplan now free-tier led), but I found no published outcome data on LTD cohorts. Peeqback: $19/mo or $149 lifetime (about 8 months of price) with a 7-day refund. No LTD found for Upvoty, Nolt, featureOS. Caveat: LTDs trade future MRR for cash and support load; for a hosted per-request service with AI/screenshot storage costs, a capped "founding member" offer is safer than true lifetime.
- **Indie $ benchmark:** Aden's own context: indie devs mostly pay under $30/mo for tools; at $29+/mo per app you are in Featurebase/UserJot/Nolt range.

## 4. Gaps and positioning

**Where YouAsked is clearly different**
- In-app, chat-style Ask sheet themed from shadcn tokens, many entry points (AskBar, FeatureCard, AskAbout, AskForIt on empty search), not an iframe/portal. Nobody in the table does contextual entry points like AskForIt.
- Loop closing both ways: requester replies in-app and "you asked, it's live" to every voter plus "What's new" sheet. Competitors mostly use email.
- Agent-first: `npx youasked init`, MCP, llms.txt, install.md, Claude Code skill, "closes YA-12" commit/PR auto-ship. Competing MCPs exist (Featurebase from May 2026 on paid plans, Canny, Quackback Pro, Upvoty Hyper, features.vote, SeggWat, openheard), so MCP alone is NOT unique; the combination of auto-ship from git plus init CLI is.
- Open source (MIT packages, FSL server) on Cloudflare; Quackback/openheard are AGPL.
- Telegram bot with buttons (nobody else surfaced).

**Where we are behind (parity list to have before launch)**
1. Public board/page + roadmap view and public changelog (nearly everyone has all three). We have FeatureBoard; confirm roadmap + changelog page.
2. Duplicate merge (have Telegram Merge) and AI duplicate suggestions at submit time. AI dedupe is table stakes at Canny/Featurebase/ProductBridge.
3. Custom domain / branded hosted page (Starter tiers of Sleekplan, Upvoty, FeedBear).
4. SSO/identity: "identify user" from host auth (JWT/HMAC) so votes are tied to app users without a second login. This is Canny's and Nolt's core. Verify we have secure identified-user mode.
5. Integrations: Linear + GitHub issue sync (Linear is expected), Slack/Discord (have), email notifications fallback for users who are not in-app.
6. Private/internal notes, tags, statuses, search, moderation/spam (Turnstile, rate limits).
7. Data export (CSV/JSON) and an API; import from Canny/Featurebase/Fider CSV (switching story).
8. Mobile: React Native and Swift SDK. Only WishKit, features.vote and pick a feature offer native, so shipping RN first would be a clear win for the target (Expo devs, like Aden).
9. Pricing page with a no-surprise, no-per-user promise; startup/nonprofit discount.

**3-5 features that would make it obviously better for indie/vibecoder devs**
1. **Agent "triage and ship" loop**: MCP + GitHub already, add "agent picks the top request, opens a draft PR, links `closes YA-n`, drafts the changelog and the 'you asked' message". Make the demo a 60-second video.
2. **React Native/Expo and SwiftUI SDK** with the same Ask sheet (native, no WebView). Near-empty field.
3. **Social listening to board** (Reddit, App Store reviews, X) with AI triage and one-click add; App Store review import is the strongest wedge for mobile indies (no board tool does it; Canny's Autopilot only pulls from Intercom/Zendesk/Gong).
4. **Zero-config shadcn theming + one-command install + free "preview" in the dev's own app** (already a strength; lead with it).
5. **Revenue-agnostic plain voting plus requester-facing proof** (public "N people asked, shipped Tuesday" cards the dev can post to X). Fits the viral "brag" angle.

## 5. Free or not

**Evidence**
- In this niche the mix is: free tiers (Canny, Featurebase, UserJot, Sleekplan, Supahub, Quackback, Userback-limited, Fider, WishKit, ProductBridge) vs trial-only (Frill, Nolt, Upvoty, FeedBear, Rapidr, featureOS, features.vote, Usersnap, Feedbucket, UserVoice). Both survive. Notably the most recently priced entrants (Featurebase Dec 2025, Canny Jun 2025) kept a free tier but made it tight (Featurebase free = 1 seat, no AI/API/MCP; Canny 25 tracked users) and moved the value into paid walls. Userback's free plan hides feedback after 7 days: a "paywall on your own data" tactic.
- Sleekplan's trial falls back to a free plan; Featurebase has a startup program of 80-86% off instead of a bigger free tier: i.e., discounts for paying cohorts, not wide free.
- Generic SaaS benchmarks: freemium free-to-paid ~2-5%; opt-in no-card trials ~8-25% trial-to-paid (varies by study: 18.2% in a 2025 study vs 8.9% in 2026 data, per aggregators; secondary sources): https://dodopayments.com/blogs/saas-free-trial-vs-freemium , https://ladderfunnel.com/blog/saas-free-trial-conversion-rate. Card-required trials convert far higher (~30-50%) at lower sign-up volume.
- I found no published conversion data from Canny/Featurebase specifically.
- Special risk for YouAsked: free tiers cost real money here (hosted AI, screenshots, notifications), and the audience (vibecoders) includes many throwaway apps; free users also provide the OSS/self-host escape hatch anyway (packages MIT, server FSL, allowing self-host for internal use).

**Recommendation for a no-free-tier launch**
- Do not go "free forever". Offer a **14-day free trial, no card** (matches Frill/Upvoty/UserJot/FeedBear norm), with the whole product on. After trial: board goes read-only and widget shows "paused", data kept (Featurebase/featureOS-style pause) for 30-60 days. This avoids deleting data and gives a re-activation path.
- **Price:** one simple indie plan around **$12-19/mo per workspace (or ~$9 on yearly)**, flat, with unlimited end users, voters and requests. A second "Team/Pro" at ~$29-39 for Linear/SSO/custom domain/higher AI limits. Yearly = **2 months free** (the dominant norm; ~17%). This undercuts Canny/Featurebase/UserJot/Nolt and sits near WishKit/Sleekplan/Peeqback while being agent-first.
- **Launch tactic:** a capped "founding member" deal (first 100 buyers: locked price for life as long as subscribed; or yearly at 4 months free) rather than true lifetime. If you want cash, a $149-199 lifetime like Peeqback's but capped to ~50 seats and excluding AI/listening add-ons.
- **Cheap top-of-funnel without a free tier:** a free open-source self-host path (already MIT/FSL), a live demo board, and a "free for open-source projects and for students" discount code, plus a startup discount. Offer a public read-only "YouAsked eats its own dog food" board.
- **Meter AI/social listening separately** (credits) so base plan stays flat and unabusable; avoid per-seat and per-tracked-user pricing, which is the most complained-about pattern (section 2).
- Revisit a tiny free tier (1 app, 25 requests, branding on) only if signups are the bottleneck after 60 days; it is cheap to add and hard to remove (Canny/Featurebase have both trimmed theirs).
